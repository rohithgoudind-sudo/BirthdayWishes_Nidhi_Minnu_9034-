Chapter 03 · From my heart

A little letter

Dear Minnu,

Six years… and through every one of them, one name has stayed quietly written across my heart — yours.

There are so many feelings I've carried in silence, things I never found brave enough words for. You were always the reason behind them.

I won't deny it — I wished, more than once, that you could be mine. But somewhere along the way, I learned that loving you also meant honoring what you chose, even when it wasn't me. And strangely, that too became a way of loving you.

So today, on your birthday, I don't ask for much. Just this — that your life stays wrapped in happiness, that every day finds a reason to make you smile.

Time will keep moving, seasons will keep changing… but the respect and quiet love I hold for you in my heart will remain exactly as it is. Untouched. Unshaken. ❤️

Happy Birthday, Nidhi… 🎂
